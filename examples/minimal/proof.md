# Finite union example

All sets below are finite, and `|X|` denotes finite cardinality.

## Lemma

If `X` and `Y` are disjoint, their tagged copies partition `X union Y`.
Therefore counting the two copies gives `|X union Y| = |X| + |Y|`.

## Theorem

For disjoint finite sets `X` and `Y`, `|X union Y| = |X| + |Y|`.
This is exactly the conclusion of the lemma.
